# Brand Strategy

## Brand promise

## Positioning
A LINE sticker family built around real work communication, distinctive character IP, and workflow-driven captions.

## Differentiation
- Workflow-first rather than generic greetings
- Torn-paper visual language
- Reusable character masters across many sets
- Utility + relatable workplace humor
- Multi-set product ladder

## Tone
Professional, friendly, concise, relatable, with controlled deadpan humor.

## Brand safety
Humor may target workload and office friction, but must not ridicule citizens, victims, legitimate duty, or protected groups.
