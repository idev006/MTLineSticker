# Character Bible v0.5 — พี่พร้อม: Paper-Tear Mascot

## Status
Working character direction. This document supersedes the generic chibi direction for further exploration. Do not begin full 40-sticker production until the master character passes the recognition gate.

## Core concept
พี่พร้อม is an administrative police character built from deliberately rough torn-paper collage. The character is not merely placed on torn paper; the body, face, hair, uniform and props themselves must visibly be constructed from irregular paper scraps.

**Character hook:** ตัวเล็ก งานใหญ่ หน้านิ่ง แต่เอาอยู่

**Internal brand DNA:** พร้อมครับ...ประมาณหนึ่ง

## Personality
- สุภาพและรับผิดชอบ
- คล่องงานและพร้อมช่วยเหลือ
- ใจเย็นภายใต้ความวุ่นวาย
- เหนื่อยได้ แต่ไม่ทิ้งงาน
- humor แบบ deadpan มากกว่าการโวยวาย
- ไม่ล้อเลียนประชาชน หน้าที่ราชการ หรือความผิดพลาดร้ายแรง

## Visual DNA
- compact, short, slightly blocky silhouette
- head built from 2–3 visible torn-paper layers
- small half-lidded eyes as the default expression
- thick torn-paper eyebrows with strong expressive range
- black hair made from 4–5 large irregular paper masses, not anime strands
- slightly oversized hands for readable gestures
- irregular deckled edges and visible paper fibers
- warm kraft/brown uniform palette with restrained yellow accent

## Signature prop
**READY Folder** — mustard-yellow rigid paper folder with torn edges, handmade/collage lettering and one recurring clip/tape detail. It is a supporting brand asset, not a substitute for recognisable facial/silhouette design.

## Behavioral hook
The default joke is contrast: พี่พร้อม remains calm and deadpan while work chaos escalates around him—paper piles, urgent notes, calls, deadlines and meetings.

## Expression language
Primary controls are eyebrows, eyelids and mouth corners. Avoid generic giant anime eyes. For stronger emotion, use paper-specific metaphors such as creases, folds, scraps flying, tape peeling or layers shifting.

## Paper-tear construction rules
1. Edges must look physically torn, not vector-perfect.
2. Major shapes should be asymmetric.
3. Layer overlap must remain visible.
4. Texture should survive sticker-size reduction without becoming noisy.
5. Do not use excessive micro-detail.
6. Maintain the same head, brow, eye, hair and proportion system across all poses.

## Recognition gate
The master character must pass:
- **Silhouette test:** recognisable without interior detail.
- **De-uniform test:** still recognisable in plain clothing.
- **Face-crop test:** head/face alone retains identity.
- **Behavior test:** deadpan-under-chaos feels specific to the IP.
- **Tiny-size test:** face and gesture remain readable at LINE thumbnail scale.

## Do not
- generic round-face big-eye chibi
- realistic police insignia as the primary identity hook
- perfectly clean digital cut edges
- props doing all the identity work
- heroic/muscular police stereotype
- overly cute childlike proportions
- humor that undermines public service responsibility

## Master lock requirement
No full 40-sticker production until Character Master Sheet + Prop Sheet + Material Guide pass the recognition and tiny-size gates.
