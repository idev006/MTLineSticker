# Caption Guidelines

One sticker = one communication intent.

Captions should be short, natural Thai, immediately readable, professional enough for work, and friendly rather than stiff.

Evaluate: frequency, breadth, workflow relevance, clarity, emotional value, brand distinctiveness and safety.

Before adding a caption to another set, check data/CAPTION_REGISTRY.csv.

Final Thai spelling, punctuation and diacritics require manual QA.
