VISUAL COMMUNICATION RULES
- Build each Frame from the approved caption, intent and chat situation.
- Meaning should be understood quickly from expression, gesture/action, caption and only the props needed for clarity.
- Apply the internal 1-second test at small/chat-like viewing size.
- Remove decorative detail that slows message recognition.
- Keep face, gesture/action and caption hierarchy readable.
- Avoid scene-heavy, poster-like or infographic-like treatment unless the approved brief explicitly requires it.
- Camera angle serves communication and Character appeal, not novelty.
- Prefer compositions that keep the Character large enough to read and the required action unmistakable.
- Use exact approved caption wording; do not rewrite upstream text.
- AI-rendered Thai text is candidate-level until visually verified; if typography will be handled downstream, leave sufficient composition space and do not claim final text accuracy.
