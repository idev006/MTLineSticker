# Universal White Die-Cut Silhouette Standard v1.3.0
Status: OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-21; MANDATORY for every newly produced sticker frame, irrespective of set.

- Sticker has a white opaque (#FFFFFF, alpha=255) shaped die-cut underlay that extends outward from the composite sticker silhouette approximately 3-5 pixels at nominal 592x512 frame scale. This is NOT a rectangular white fill, and not the black frame border.
- Build from the union of all actual sticker elements meant to travel with the sticker, including character, approved garment marks, props, intentional effects, and sticker shadows. Apply appropriate morphological dilation/outline with rounded joins to avoid islands and seams. The white border must be continuous around each connected sticker silhouette, without unintended gaps, holes, abrupt corners or clipped edges. For intentionally disconnected visual elements, each component needs its own complete white underlay or must be deliberately joined for cut-safe export.
- Place white die-cut BEHIND art, never paint over details. Background outside underlay and other sticker elements remains truly transparent: RGBA alpha=0. Do not draw faux checkerboard or export RGB without alpha.
- The die-cut OUTERMOST white pixels count as sticker content for all safe-zone / clipping checks. Keep every part at least the selected safe clearance from the INNER EDGE of the opaque 3px frame border, with nominal clearance 15-30px and +/-5% of selected target. Prefer 20px safe clearance. Never let white touch the black frame.
- Canonical 4x2 sheet, nominal 592x512 frame ratio 592:512, black 3px frame, transparent 15px inter-frame gap, governed by geometry standard v1.2.0. At transformed frame scales, scale die-cut accordingly; actual cutter interface must be confirmed.
- Deterministic compositor must generate and verify white silhouette with pixel masks (Pillow/OpenCV or equivalent), rather than relying on a generative image model to assert precise alpha, radius or thickness.
- QA per frame: test contour background alpha, die-cut solid white alpha=255 where visible, approximate 3-5px extension away from subject, continuity, no visible detached white artifacts, no shape collision with frame border, no unexpected white rectangular canvas; test cutter produces clean sticker with die-cut after frame removal.
- If segmentation, source alpha, compositor, or end-to-end cutter evidence unavailable, mark technical QA NOT RUN or BLOCKED. Do not certify sticker as cut-ready from preview alone.
