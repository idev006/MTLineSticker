# Torn-Paper Material Guide

Phi Prom is constructed from torn paper; torn paper is not merely a background effect.

- Skin: warm cream/kraft paper
- Hair/brows: rough black paper
- Uniform: layered brown papers
- Details: cream, gray, mustard paper
- Visible fibers, irregular edges, layered overlap
- Avoid perfect vector edges
- Keep texture readable after reduction

Candidate sheets may use a flat high-contrast removable background. Final LINE assets require transparent background.
