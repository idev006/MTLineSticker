# Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Launch Objective
Prove that workflow-specific police administrative stickers are sent repeatedly and generate peer discovery.

## 2. Core Marketing Message
**ใช้ได้จริง เพราะมาจากชีวิตงานจริง**

Secondary line:
**รับงาน ประสาน ตาม ส่ง — แล้วพักบ้าง**

## 3. Acquisition Hypothesis
The most efficient discovery may occur inside existing work/friend groups when a user sends a distinctive sticker and peers recognize the occupational context.

## 4. Launch Creative Angles
### Utility
“40 คำที่คนสายอำนวยการหยิบใช้ได้จริง”

### Recognition
“ถ้าคุณเคยตามข้อมูล รอลงนาม และโดนงานแทรก ชุดนี้พูดแทนคุณได้”

### Humor
Feature safe signatures such as:
- งานเข้าครับ!
- ขอพักแป๊บ
- รับทราบ...แต่ไม่รับเรื่อง

## 5. Content Strategy
Pre-launch:
- character reveal
- 8 prototype preview
- “หนึ่งวันสายอำนวยการ” carousel/post concept
- audience polling on missing phrases

Launch:
- show the utility range, not only cute art
- demonstrate realistic chat situations
- highlight 3–5 signature stickers

Post-launch:
- collect user comments about frequently used/missing phrases
- identify screenshots/social mentions if available
- use data/feedback to shape Set 02

## 6. Product-Led Growth Loop
Purchase → send → peer sees → recognition → product discovery → purchase → more sending.

Optimize art and captions for this loop.

## 7. KPI Framework
Primary:
- sales / purchases
- send count (where available)
- repeat sending indicators / top sent stickers

Diagnostic:
- top 10 most-used captions
- bottom 10
- utility vs humor performance
- professional vs friendly vs humor performance
- feedback on missing phrases

## 8. Experiment Plan
### A — Hero caption
Compare utility-led promotional creative vs humor-led promotional creative.

### B — Character expression
Test calmer professional expression vs more exaggerated relatable expression.

### C — Naming
Test occupation-specific title vs broader “สายอำนวยการ” title for store discoverability and audience comfort.

### D — Follow-on Set
Use usage/feedback evidence to choose Set 02 between Meetings & Coordination or Follow-up & Deadlines.

## 9. Brand Safety
Never market using claims of official endorsement, official police affiliation, or representation unless such rights/approval genuinely exist.

## 10. Long-Term Expansion
If the IP earns traction, extend to:
- female administrative character
- meetings & coordination
- follow-up & deadlines
- documents & reporting
- workplace humor
- other public-service/workplace professions under a broader master brand
