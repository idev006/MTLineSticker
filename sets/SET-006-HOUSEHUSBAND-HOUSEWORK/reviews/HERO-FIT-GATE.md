## Acceptance criteria
A hero prototype passes only when all of the following are true:
1. Character identity matches the approved orange-shoes Character Sheet.
2. Clean-shaven face and military/police-inspired short haircut are preserved.
3. Navy shirt, black shorts, orange shoes and left-wrist tactical watch remain consistent where visible.
4. Black crossbody bag is used only when it does not interfere with the housework action.
5. Pose communicates the intent before reading the caption.
6. Caption is exact Thai, legible and natural.
7. Background is truly transparent PNG.
8. Safe area and composition survive reduction to LINE delivery size.
9. Props support the action without overwhelming the character.
10. The six heroes look like one commercial sticker product and remain distinct from Set 1.

## Prototype round 1 audit
**Result: FAIL / REJECTED — not accepted into production assets.**
