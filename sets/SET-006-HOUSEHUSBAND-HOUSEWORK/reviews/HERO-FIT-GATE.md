## Golden Reference
H01 “ล้างจานอยู่ครับ” approved by the owner is the **Hero Golden Reference** for SET-006.

It establishes the accepted combination of:
- adult semi-anime face and proportions,
- very-short police/military-inspired haircut,
- clean-shaven face,
- navy shirt,
- black shorts when visible,
- orange shoes when visible,
- black left-wrist tactical watch,
- black crossbody bag when it does not obstruct the action,
- bold readable Thai caption,
- simplified housework action/prop storytelling,
- sticker-first composition rather than scene/infographic composition.

The Golden Reference complements, but does not replace, the approved Character Sheet.

## Acceptance criteria — every Hero
A Hero passes only when all are true:
1. Character identity matches the approved Character Sheet and H01 Golden Reference.
2. Adult proportions are preserved; no unapproved chibi conversion.
3. Clean-shaven face and locked very-short haircut are preserved.
4. Signature wardrobe/accessories remain consistent where visible.
5. One primary communication intent is obvious.
6. Candidate passes the **1-second communication test** at chat-preview scale.
7. Pose/expression/action supports the exact caption.
8. Props clarify the message and do not turn the asset into a scene illustration.
9. Thai wording is exact and legible; AI text is candidate-only until verified.
10. Individual final master has real alpha transparency.
11. Safe area and composition survive LINE-size reduction.
12. Candidate is a standalone sticker asset, not a poster, infographic or multi-panel artwork.
