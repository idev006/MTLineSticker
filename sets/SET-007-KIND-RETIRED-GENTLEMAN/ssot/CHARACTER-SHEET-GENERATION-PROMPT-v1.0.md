# CHARACTER SHEET GENERATION PROMPT v1.0

## Role
You are a senior character designer and LINE sticker production artist.

## Objective
Create a professional Character Sheet Candidate for a Thai mature male character based on the approved reference photos and the SSOT documents in this set.

## Character Essence
“ผู้ใหญ่ใจดี เกษียณแล้วก็ยังเท่” — warm, polite, dignified, healthy, active, approachable, mature but not old-looking or frail.

## Visual Requirements
- Preserve recognizable likeness from the reference photos
- Warm semi-chibi sticker style with mature adult proportions
- Slightly enlarged head for expression readability, but retain adult torso/limb proportions
- Round/soft face, very short neat hair, friendly eyes, natural warm smile
- Healthy upright posture, confident and relaxed body language
- Main outfit: clean grey sporty jacket/tracksuit with simple white sleeve details, dark inner shirt, coordinated dark trousers, simple sporty footwear
- Premium, clean, uncluttered rendering

## Required Sheet Content
- full-body front
- 3/4 left
- 3/4 right
- side
- back
- face front close-up
- face 3/4 close-up
- 10-expression panel
- 8-gesture panel
- outfit detail panel
- color palette swatches

## Age Treatment
The character must clearly read as an experienced mature adult who takes care of himself. Do not visually age him through excessive wrinkles, sagging skin, grey hair emphasis, hunched posture, fatigue, or frailty. Keep dignity, vitality, warmth, and attractiveness.

## Expression Direction
Expressions should be clear enough for LINE sticker scale while remaining polite and believable. Avoid exaggerated slapstick.

## Background
Neutral, simple review background with clear separation of all views. No decorative scenery.

## Fidelity Priority
1. face shape
2. hair silhouette
3. smile and eyes
4. mature healthy body silhouette
5. outfit identity

## Output Intent
This is a **Candidate**, not yet the final active SSOT. It will be reviewed for likeness, age, proportion, outfit, expression clarity, and consistency before approval.
