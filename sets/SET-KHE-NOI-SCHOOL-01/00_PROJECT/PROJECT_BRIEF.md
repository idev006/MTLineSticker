# Project Brief

Project ID: SET-KHE-NOI-SCHOOL-01
Thai title: เข้น้อย อยู่โรงเรียน No.01 (image spells เข้น้อย; text originally said แข่น้อย, working title pending final confirmation)
English: Khe Noi at Police Training School #01
Product: 40 distinct LINE stickers, five sheets of eight, comedic training-school experiences.
Audience: police cadets, alumni, teachers and families; captions must also function in everyday chats.
Story world: fictional provincial police training center, dawn formation, drill, sprints, carrying equipment, personal kit care, meals, rest and camaraderie.
Key message: เหนื่อยจริง ฮาจริง ไม่ทิ้งเพื่อน; humor with empathy, no glorification of violence or unsafe punishment.
Product owner supplied an image of crocodile-headed character in white MT 007 V-neck, black shorts and shoes, wristwatch left wrist, multi-angle view. Deliver same exact identity, do not redraw a new mascot.
Constraints: Thai legible captions, distinct jokes and visual verbs, true alpha, line import geometry per latest Production Contract v4.
Acceptance: 40 distinct records, 5×8 unique slots, no visual identity drift, owner lock and A1 pass before B.
