## Production Rule Going Forward
Full Production review format:
- 4 sheets
- 10 frames per sheet
- 5 columns × 2 rows
- master target 512×512 per review frame
- 20 px safe padding target
- transparent background for artwork
- review border allowed only on review sheet
- final exports have no border

## Sticker-First Correction
For every frame:
Character > Expression/Gesture > Caption > Primary Prop > Accent

Avoid:
- scenic background
- decorative clusters
- full greeting-card look
- excessive flowers/hearts/sparkles
- props larger than necessary
