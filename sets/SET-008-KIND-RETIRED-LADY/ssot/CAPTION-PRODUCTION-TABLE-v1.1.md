## Portfolio Intent
- 7 weekday greetings are mandatory and locked
- Daily utility increased by giving several reaction poses concise captions
- Props remain restrained and secondary
- Character must dominate every frame
- Scenic backgrounds are prohibited unless specifically approved

## QA Notes
- Thai spelling and tone marks must be checked frame-by-frame
- Long weekday captions may use two-level typography
- If text becomes too small, reduce decoration before reducing legibility
