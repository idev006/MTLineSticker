# Character Bible — CHAICHAT #1 / ท่านเตอร์

## Identity locks
**CB-01** Subject reference: man on right of the most recent flower-garden couple photo, white baseball cap worn backward. Discard superseded orange-polo uniformed police reference for this set.

**CB-02** Face: faithfully retain brow, eye shape, nose, mouth, jaw, natural adult facial proportions and recognizable identity. Skin smooth, avoid prominent wrinkles; do not turn into a child or alter ethnic features.

**CB-03** Proportions: oversized head, notably compact/slim body, caricature-like stylization. Attractive professional cartoon model, not babyish chibi. Keep head-body ratio consistent in all views and frames.

**CB-04** Costume: white backward baseball cap, dark navy padded/puffer jacket, grey zipped midlayer, dark trousers, white trainers. No police uniform or peaked cap from previous concept; no CHAICHAT sports tracksuit unless owner explicitly reapproves.

**CB-05** Personality: calm, confident, warm, considerate, composed and credible adult; subtle humor permitted. Do not overplay distorted eyes/mouth, toddler stances or humiliating caricature.

**CB-06** Text policy: no captions, dialogue, floating words or numerals as speech in all 40 final sticker artworks. Decorative iconography such as hearts, clock, ? and checkmarks only when needed and legible. Avoid unnecessary branding on costume.

**CB-07** Expression: one primary communication intent per frame, clear at chat thumbnail size, with restrained but readable expression and dynamic enough gesture.

**CB-08** Continuity: match facial identity, cap orientation, clothing color, hairstyle, skin tone, hands and footwear across poses. Same character, not lookalikes.

## Character Sheet specification
Produce front, 3/4, side, and back full-body turnaround; close-up face angles; expressions (greeting, smiling, stern, uncertain, thoughtful, appreciative), and representative actions (wave, thumbs-up, stop, watch-check, busy paperwork). Keep a neutral plain or transparent presentation background. This is a planning/specification document, not a newly approved rendering.

## Negative constraints
No photo-real human, no infant body language, no deep wrinkles, no exaggerated cheek puff, no gigantic anime eyes, no uniform substitution, no new badges/rank, no caption bubbles, no text other than labels outside artwork, no multiple inconsistent models.
